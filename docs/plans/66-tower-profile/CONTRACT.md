## Method

Instrument: nsys on the pod image, proven by a kernel-table-bearing probe before the legs. Census: N/M pairs, sqlite export, `kernel_census.py` per-`(kernel, grid, block)` difference. Wall: `(wall_M − wall_N)/(M−N)`; **front end**: `(front_M − front_N)/(M−N)` from the P1-b timer (a direct measurement, not `wall − busy`); `gpu_busy` from the census; `launch/sync residual = wall − front − busy`, reported.

Attribution (kernel grain, per tower): C-LORA (D2 delta + `lora_linear_fused` kernels by name), C-LN (`layer_norm_fused` by name; eager LN by shape interval as in #356), C-GELU (HTSAT after P1-a: `gelu_erf_fused` by name; CLIP: `quick_gelu` elementwise kernels by shape `[rows·S, 4·width]`), **C-ATTN-<tower>** (element-count signatures at `rows = 24`: scores/probs `[24, h, S, S]`, S = 77 text / 50 vision / per-window for HTSAT (`[B·nW, heads, ws², ws²]`) — HTSAT's attention is attributed, its port decision is out of scope), UNATTRIBUTED = the rest.

Positive proof (dynamic admission, post-#460): per admitted key `fused + eager == calls × batches` with the split REPORTED; a decision leg additionally requires `fused > 0` for `lora_linear_fused` and `layer_norm_fused` (the towers' domain predicates admit on contiguous tensors; a non-zero `eager` share is reported, never hidden); D1/D2 require `fused == 0` for every disabled key AND `--expect-kernels-disabled` satisfied. Any equation failing ⇒ leg INVALID.

Validity gate: kernel table present; counter equations hold; `--expect-kernels-disabled` satisfied on D legs; **UNATTRIBUTED ≤ 5 % of gpu_busy** (C-ATTN-HTSAT is attributed, so this is satisfiable).

## Decision rule (two-sided; per `(chain, tower)`; candidate ports only: C-ATTN-CLIP-text, C-ATTN-CLIP-vision, C-MLP-CLIP-text, C-MLP-CLIP-vision)

With `s_wall = share_wall(C)`, `s_busy = share_gpu_busy(C)`, `U` = UNATTRIBUTED share (same denominator as the clause):
- **ACTIVATE** iff `s_wall ≥ 10 %` on ≥ 1 decision-grade decision leg (A1 or A2) of THAT tower.
- **DECLINE** iff `s_wall + U_wall < 5 %` AND `s_busy + U_busy < 5 %` on EVERY decision-grade decision leg of that tower (a CPU-front-end-bound wall cannot license a kernel DECLINE by itself).
- **UNRESOLVED** otherwise (incl. the 5–10 % band; A1 not decision-grade ⇒ UNRESOLVED; A2 not decision-grade ⇒ the outcome is F32-only and says so).
- Realized-gain chains (C-LORA, C-LN, C-GELU-HTSAT) get numbers, not verdicts. C-ATTN-HTSAT gets a number and the standing "out of tier" note.
- Acceptance for an activated port: same-box A/B vs the tower's OWN eager base at the activating leg's shape, fused leg under `JAMMI_KERNELS_STRICT=1`, net wall p50 ≥ 5 %, counters `fused>0/eager=0`, eval bytes bit-identical on the same CPU model (the CLIP `MASKED_LOGIT` sentinel is the ONE declared exception, with its own proof that masked probabilities are exactly 0 under both sentinels), goldens kept.
